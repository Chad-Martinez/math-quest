# Scene and learning styles

- Match the approved composition and use layered styles scoped to the scene. Preserve character proportions, visible feet or waterline, and essential landmarks. Use supplied orientation-specific art when available.
- Check phone and iPad in portrait and landscape plus desktop. Keep dialogue, controls, focus and safe-area spacing usable. Preserve tap targets, contrast, and cues beyond color. Keep learning views steady and compact.
- Use the approved timings and reduced-motion end state. Keep nonessential motion finite and controls operable. Separate visual choreography from progression and rewards. See the [scene production standard](../docs/scene-production.md).
