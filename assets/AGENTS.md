# Graphical assets

- Use approved art and character references. Preserve geometry, palette, proportions and accessories. Report missing or unsuitable art with needed format and dimensions. Seek approval before regeneration or replacement.
- Keep only runtime art in this tree. Keep references and contact sheets in the production kit. Reuse identical approved assets by relative path. Use layered WebP or PNG environments, transparent sprite frames and individual SVG or PNG units as appropriate.
- Check alpha edges, feet or waterline, common frame anchors, aspect ratio and portrait crops. Use approved orientation variants. Baked narrative props can stay in the background. Keep dialogue, controls and mathematical quantities in live layers.
- Track source, path, dimensions, format, usage, anchors, frame order and limitations in the kit asset manifest. See [art direction and characters](../docs/art-direction.md).
