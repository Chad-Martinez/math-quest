# Curriculum

Phase 1 practices relationships among whole numbers through 20. Story Forest joins and separates groups. Racing Valley compares quantities and distinguishes tying from taking the lead. Dinosaur Island asks for an intermediate total before a final calculation. Animal Kingdom and Sports Arena support counting, category selection, numerical comparison, and selecting true statements. Equation Space treats equality as matching values and varies the missing element. Treasure Island rotates across the other skills.

Vocabulary appears in story context and hints. Encourage learners to identify the quantities, the relationship, and what the question asks. Read-aloud, drawing, and hints are optional supports. Worked examples help a learner continue and do not award a solve star.
