# Art direction and character references

The approved Scene 01 art anchors Dinosaur Island’s warm storybook style. Preserve rounded forms, bold outlines, warm shading, lush greens, turquoise water, cream panels, forest-green controls and gold accents. Use the illustrated branding and wooden signs where specified. Keep active math workspaces calm with large, recognizable objects. Compose with separate layers and semantic controls. Use the kit’s backgrounds, anchors and orientation variants. Keep nearby paths, nest and other story landmarks visible.

This inventory describes approved uses. The actual sprite or approved kit sheet governs proportions, markings, palette and accessories.

| Character | Approved reference and use |
| --- | --- |
| T-Rex | Orange skin and darker markings, cream muzzle and belly, green bandana, brown backpack and bedroll. Reuse the approved [welcome poses and walk frames](../assets/scene-01/) and [Missing Egg expressions](../assets/scene-02/) as the scene calls for. |
| Brachiosaurus, Velociraptor, Triceratops, Stegosaurus and Ankylosaurus | The [Scene 03 sprites](../assets/scene-03/) are approved for map scale. Full-resolution gameplay designs require separate approval for their new scenes. |
| Mosasaurus | The [approved teal-blue sprite](../assets/characters/mosa-leap.png) has dark markings and a cream underside. Reuse this sprite for the map. Its full-scene staging follows the approved lagoon kit. |
| Megalodon | The Chapter 1 kit includes the blue-gray character reference with a cream underside. Major appearance is deferred to Chapter 2. |

The island is fictional. Mosasaurus and Megalodon are prehistoric marine animals. Record missing art, crop or alpha limitations and the requested replacement dimensions during review. Reference sheets belong in the kit.
